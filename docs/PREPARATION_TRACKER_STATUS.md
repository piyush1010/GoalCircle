## Full tracker delivered (3 Nov 2026, branch `prep-tracker-full`)
What changed from Milestone 2, following the owner's direction:
- **Progress comes from ticking subtopics.** 470 official UPSC subtopics (`202611080001`), broken down from the notification the way standard books and coaching syllabus guides split it. A topic's study share = ticked official subtopics / total. Private subtopics are allowed but do not count.
- **Scoring v2 (`lib/prep/scoring/v2.ts`) with decay.** Each started topic scores study 40% + practice 30% + revision 30%. After a revision falls due, the topic's strength fades linearly over max(14 days, 2 × interval) to a 25% floor, so not revising lowers overall preparation; revising restores it. Coverage never fades. Cap 95, "Not enough evidence" under 5 topics.
- **State exams (`202611080002`, generated by `supabase/seed/state_pcs_v1.py`).** UPPCS (125 topics, 585 subtopics, incl. GS V and GS VI on Uttar Pradesh, General Hindi, the CSAT English/Hindi sections), BPSC (60 topics, 368 subtopics, single Prelims GS paper, Mains GS I/II, Hindi, Essay, qualifying optional), JKPSC CCE (163 topics, 527 subtopics, J&K history, geography, economy, governance and security, two optionals). Shared areas reuse the UPSC topics and subtopics; PYQ frequency left empty for these exams (not measured).
- **Time-bound plan (`lib/prep/pace.ts`, `/prep/plan`).** Exam + stage + date + hours → "N subtopics a day", which ones today and the next 6 days, on-track/behind status with the arithmetic shown, a 15% revision window at the end, and a realism check against the hours given. Recalculated daily.
- **Setup in one sheet (`components/prep/PrepSetup.tsx`)**: exam, Prelims/Mains, date (quick chips), hours, reminder.
- **Daily reminder (`202611080003`)**: hourly job at :30 UTC (= top of the India hour); one push per day at the chosen hour with today's target, revisions due and days left; skipped if the student already logged that day. New notification kind `prep_reminder` → opens `/prep/plan`.
- **Come-back loop on Today**: today's target with inline ticks, revisions due with one-tap Revised, study streak, points lost to overdue revision.

## Aspirant review and additions (3 Nov 2026, branch `prep-aspirant-features`)
Looked at the tracker as a UPSC/state PCS aspirant would: what would make me open it daily, what would make me stop.
Added:
- **Daily habits on Today**: one tap for "Newspaper" and "Answer written" (no forms), plus "Mistake".
- **Mistake notebook** (`/prep/mistakes`, `202611090001`): add in ~10 seconds (type, optional topic, the right fact); "Got it" when revisited; commonest mistake types, with a note when they are exam-technique rather than knowledge gaps.
- **Weekly review** on Progress: study days, subtopics, revisions, PYQs, answers and newspaper days against last week; 7-day strip.
- **Needs attention**: topics with PYQ accuracy under 50% (10+ attempted) or 2+ open mistakes, each linking to the topic.
- **Optional subject**: chosen in setup; included in the Mains plan, other optionals hidden from the syllabus, Optional readiness uses only it.
- **Start fresh from today** when behind: re-spreads what is left instead of showing a growing deficit.
- **Rest day**: one missed day a week no longer breaks the streak.
